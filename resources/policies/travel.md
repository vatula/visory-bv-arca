# Corporate Travel and Accommodation Policy

**Effective Date:** January 2026
**Applies To:** All employees traveling for business purposes

### 1. Objective
To ensure duty of care, consolidate corporate travel spend, and maintain standardized accommodation quality.

### 2. Approved Booking Channels
All flights and standard hotel accommodations must be booked through the company's designated Corporate Travel Management (CTM) portal. Transactions originating from standard airlines (e.g., Qantas, Virgin) or approved hotel chains will be processed standardly.

### 3. Non-Standard and Peer-to-Peer Lodging
The use of peer-to-peer lodging networks, third-party aggregators, or short-term rental platforms (e.g., Airbnb, Stayz, Booking.com) is generally prohibited due to liability and insurance limitations.

### 4. Exception Workflow
If an accommodation expense from a non-standard provider (e.g., Airbnb) appears on a corporate card, it cannot be automatically cleared. The expense must be flagged, and the system must request a recorded exception approval note or email from the employee's direct Line Manager before the invoice can be coded to the `Travel & Accommodation` ledger.
