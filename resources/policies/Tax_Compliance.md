# Statutory Payments and Tax Compliance Policy

**Effective Date:** Financial Year 2026
**Applies To:** Finance and Payroll Departments

### 1. Objective
To enforce strict internal controls over payments made to government, statutory, or tax authorities, preventing duplicate payments or misallocated tax liabilities.

### 2. Definition of Statutory Payments
This includes, but is not limited to, payments made to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO), State Revenue Offices (e.g., Payroll Tax), and superannuation clearing houses.

### 3. Verification Thresholds
While minor lodgement fees or administrative penalties under $1,000 AUD may be processed standardly, any statutory payment or tax instalment exceeding **$1,000.00 AUD** (such as ATO PAYG Instalments or BAS payments) requires enhanced verification.

### 4. Mandatory Matching
Automated systems are not permitted to reconcile high-value statutory payments using bank feed descriptions alone. The payment must be strictly matched against the specific, officially lodged Notice of Assessment, Business Activity Statement (BAS), or Lodgement Receipt. If the exact supporting government document is not found in the shared repository, a registered financial controller must be alerted to manually upload and verify the document.
