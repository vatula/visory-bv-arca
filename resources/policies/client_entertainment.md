# Client Entertainment and Meals Policy

**Effective Date:** March 2025
**Applies To:** All Staff

### 1. Objective
To outline the acceptable use of corporate funds for business-related dining and client entertainment, ensuring compliance with Fringe Benefits Tax (FBT) regulations.

### 2. Routine Meals
Routine employee meals (e.g., office lunches, working late meals via platforms like Uber Eats or Deliveroo) under $100 AUD do not require itemized attendee lists, provided a valid receipt is submitted.

### 3. Entertainment Expenditures
Any dining, restaurant, or entertainment expense equal to or exceeding **$100.00 AUD** (e.g., cafe or restaurant bookings) is strictly classified under Client/Team Entertainment. 

### 4. FBT Compliance and Substantiation
For FBT assessment purposes, any expense flagged under Section 3 must be substantiated with:
* An itemized tax receipt.
* A complete list of all attendees (both internal employees and external clients).
* A brief description of the business discussed.
If this information is missing from the submitted documentation, reconciliation must be paused until the cardholder provides the attendee details.
